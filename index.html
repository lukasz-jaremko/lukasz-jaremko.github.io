Profile

Deep molecular science with a discovery mindset.

My research focuses on the fundamental molecular machinery of life—proteins and nucleic acids— how they function, interact with small molecules and change in disease. I work especially on difficult molecular systems where dynamics, transient interactions, hidden binding sites and heterogeneous states determine function and druggability.

I combine biomolecular NMR, biophysics, chemical biology, metabolomics and computation to characterize dynamics and interactions, track and identify small molecules, and analyze complex molecular systems. The goal is practical: understand mechanism, discover and design ligands, evaluate what is experimentally supported and determine what should be tested next.

Scientific focus

Mechanisms, molecular recognition and tractable opportunities.

01

Cancer & epigenetic therapeutics

Disease-driving mechanisms, chromatin regulators and molecular recognition in cancer, with selective chemical modulation to generate probes, validate targets and advance epigenetic drug-discovery opportunities.

02

Difficult targets & cryptic binding sites

Structural dynamics and biophysics to reveal cryptic binding sites, validate tractability and expand the druggable target space for chemical-probe and drug-candidate discovery.

03

Protein dynamics & aggregation

Flexible proteins, intrinsically disordered systems and oligomeric states—including tau and α-synuclein—with emphasis on mechanism, inhibitors, fluorescent probes and molecular intervention in neurodegeneration.

04

NMR, biophysics & molecular analysis

Biomolecular NMR, orthogonal biophysics, metabolomics and computation for structure, dynamics and ligand binding, including methods to detect, track and identify small molecules and their interactions in complex biological systems.

05

Integrated drug discovery

Target validation, assay strategy, screening, structural and biophysical evidence, ligand characterization and experimental de-risking—from mechanism to chemical probes and drug-candidate decisions.

06

Scientific technology development

Developing and integrating spectroscopic, analytical and computational approaches that improve how molecular systems are measured, interpreted and translated into discovery, diagnostic and technology applications.

Scientific assessment

I assess the science behind targets, technologies and discovery programs.

My approach is evidence-driven: define the mechanism, test the assumptions, identify scientific risk and determine which experiment or analysis will change the decision. I have also worked with companies on scientific analysis, feasibility, applicability and the practical limits of proposed technologies and research directions.
