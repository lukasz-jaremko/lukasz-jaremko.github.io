Profile

From molecular mechanism to intervention.

My research focuses on the fundamental molecular machinery of life—proteins and nucleic acids— how they interact with small molecules, and how these interactions can be understood and modulated to create new therapeutic and diagnostic opportunities.

I combine structural biology, chemical biology, biomolecular biophysics, metabolomics and computation. I develop and apply NMR spectroscopy to characterize biomolecular dynamics and interactions, track and identify small molecules, and analyze complex molecular mixtures. These methods are integrated with biochemical, structural, biophysical and computational approaches to reveal disease mechanisms, discover and design ligands, and build practical molecular-analysis workflows.

Research focus

Turning difficult molecular questions into actionable opportunities.

Cancer mechanisms & epigenetic drug discovery

Defining disease mechanisms and druggable vulnerabilities in cancer, including epigenetic targets and cryptic binding sites, to expand the druggable target space and guide the development of chemical probes and drug candidates.

Dynamic & difficult protein targets

Studying flexible proteins, intrinsically disordered systems and oligomeric states—including tau and α-synuclein—to identify molecular intervention strategies, inhibitors and fluorescent probes for neurodegeneration and related diseases.

Molecular detection & analysis

Developing NMR and computational methods to detect, identify and track small molecules, characterize their interactions, and extract useful information from complex biological mixtures for metabolomics, diagnostics, natural products and environmental research.

Integrated molecular discovery

Combining experiment with computational and AI-enabled analysis to move from molecular mechanism and measurement toward validated ligands, probes, biomarkers and discovery workflows.

I help turn difficult molecular targets and complex molecular data into experimentally validated opportunities for drug discovery, chemical-probe development and diagnostics.

Scientific leadership

Building research groups, collaborations and scientific capability.

Since 2017, I have led independent research groups and interdisciplinary projects, recruited and mentored scientists, and contributed to the development of shared NMR, structural-biology, proteomics and metabolomics capabilities.

I build and sustain collaborations across universities, research institutes, hospitals, technology providers, biotechnology and pharmaceutical environments, connecting scientific expertise with practical research and translational goals.
