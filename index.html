Profile

Molecular mechanisms, dynamics and discovery.

My research examines how proteins and nucleic acids function, interact with small molecules and change in molecularly complex and difficult-to-treat diseases. I focus on difficult systems in which conformational dynamics, transient interactions, hidden binding sites and heterogeneous states govern molecular function and druggability.

By integrating structural, spectroscopic, biophysical, chemical and computational approaches, I define molecular mechanisms, characterize ligand interactions and identify the experiments most likely to reduce uncertainty.

Methods NMR spectroscopy · X-ray crystallography · cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) · biophysical chemistry · chemical biology · computational structural modelling

Scientific focus

Mechanisms, molecular recognition and tractable opportunities.

Cancer & epigenetic therapeutics

Molecular mechanisms, chromatin regulators and therapeutic vulnerabilities in oncology, with particular experience in lung and pancreatic cancer. Selective chemical modulation is used to validate targets, define molecular recognition and inform epigenetic drug discovery.

Difficult targets & cryptic binding sites

Structural dynamics and biophysics to reveal cryptic binding sites, assess tractability and identify new opportunities for chemical-probe and drug-candidate discovery.

Protein dynamics & aggregation

Flexible proteins, intrinsically disordered systems and oligomeric states—including tau and α-synuclein—with emphasis on mechanism and applications toward inhibitor and fluorescent-probe development in neurodegenerative disease.

Structural methods & molecular analysis

NMR spectroscopy, X-ray crystallography, cryo-EM, complementary biophysics, and computational structural modelling to define molecular structure, conformational dynamics, interaction mechanisms and ligand binding. Metabolomics and complementary analytical workflows support detection, tracking and identification of small molecules in complex biological systems.

Integrated drug discovery

Target validation, assay strategy, screening, structural and biophysical evidence, ligand characterization and experimental prioritization—from mechanism to chemical probes and drug-candidate decisions.

Methods & technology development

Developing and integrating spectroscopic, analytical and computational methods that improve how molecular systems are measured, interpreted and applied in discovery, diagnostics and biotechnology.
